Hauptaufgaben

  • Define and drive the product roadmap for shopping capabilities within Gemini, identifying user needs and high-impact opportunities for conversational shopping.
  • Design experiences that delight consumers while maintaining a healthy, sustainable ecosystem that creates value for merchants, retailers, and creators of all sizes.
  • Guide features from initial concept, UX exploration, and prototyping through launch and continuous iteration at Google scale.
  • Partner with UX and Engineering to design intuitive conversational paradigms, interactive product cards, visual browsing, and agentic workflows.
  • Establish evaluation frameworks, quality benchmarks, and safety/grounding standards to ensure shopping recommendations in Gemini are accurate, unbiased, trustworthy, and actionable.
  • Define key performance indicators (engagement, user satisfaction, conversion, trust), analyze experiments, and leverage data-driven insights to accelerate product growth.
  • Partner seamlessly across Google Commerce, Gemini and Merchant platforms to drive strategic alignment, technical integration, and shared OKRs.

Wichtige Anforderungen

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.
  • 10 years of experience in product management or related technical role.
  • 5 years of experience taking technical products from conception to launch (e.g., ideation to execution, end-to-end, 0 to 1, etc.).
  • Experience integrating generative AI tools or LLM interfaces into workflows.

Weitere Details

  • Knowledge of, or continued interest in building accessibility best practices, and experience/curiosity for incorporating accessibility into product development methodologies.
  • Advertisers worldwide use Google Ads to promote their products; publishers use AdSense to serve relevant ads on their website; and business around the world use our products (like Google Shopping, and Google Wallet) to support their online businesses and bring users into their offline stores.
