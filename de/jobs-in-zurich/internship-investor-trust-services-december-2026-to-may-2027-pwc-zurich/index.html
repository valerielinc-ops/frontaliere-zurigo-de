Beschreibung

You will be part of our Investor Trust Services team within Assurance. Unlike in traditional asset management or investment banking, we don't manage client assets or make investment decisions. Instead, we support transparency, trust, and regulatory compliance by providing assurance services to asset managers, banks, and other financial institutions. Learn more about our services here.

The focus of your internship will be supporting our Assurance Valuation Desk, which performs independent valuations of financial instruments and provides specialist support to audit teams across PwC Switzerland.

  • Support audit engagements for our assurance clients in the valuation of financial instruments and derivatives.
