Social production is a macroeconomic indicator that extends the national accounts beyond GDP by placing a monetary value on unpaid socially productive activity that creates genuine social, environmental, and economic value but remains invisible in the current economic frame – including care work, volunteering, civic participation, mentoring, ecological stewardship, and contributions to open knowledge.

Hauptaufgaben

  • Your responsibilities
  • Aligning the established social production method with European System of Accounts (ESA 2010), adapting how it is applied and reported while preserving the underlying methodology.
  • Mapping the social production categories to available SAKE data, assessing coverage, and conducting a gap analysis of categories not yet captured.
  • Compiling and analysing the relevant Swiss data sources to produce the pilot account's first estimates.
  • Building the account into a working statistical product, documented and reproducible so that it could be updated on a regular cycle by Switzerland and/or other nations wishing to adopt the approach.
  • Co-authoring research and policy outputs, including a joint report on social production in Switzerland.
  • Preparing a methodological report setting out options to extend category coverage, with recommendations for Switzerland and a generalisable approach that other national statistical offices could follow.
  • Applying the resulting social production measures to examine productive contribution across the lifecourse, with particular attention to the contributions of older people and the implications for healthy longevity and measures of demographic dependency.
  • Working in person within the National Accounts Division and liaising across FSO units to resolve questions of definition, derivation, and method.
  • Maintaining reproducible workflows and documentation consistent with official statistics standards.
