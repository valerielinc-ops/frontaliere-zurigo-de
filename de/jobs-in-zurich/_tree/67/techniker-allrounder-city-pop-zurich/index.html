🔧 Technician Allrounder (100%) – City Pop | Zurich

We are looking for an experienced and versatile technician to strengthen our team in the long term. If you combine manual skills with technical know-how and enjoy working independently – then you are exactly right with us.

🛠️ Your Tasks

  • Carrying out technical repairs (electrical, manual)
  • Ongoing maintenance and inspection of our properties (including equipment, outdoor areas)
  • Rapid fault analysis and troubleshooting
  • Regular inspections of building systems (heating, ventilation, air conditioning etc.)
  • Ensuring flawless technical condition of all systems
  • General building maintenance
  • Documentation of work and reporting to supervisors
  • Collaboration with internal departments
  • Contact person for technical concerns of our customers
  • Participation in the 24/7 on-call duty in rotation
  • Compliance with emergency processes and safety standards

✅ Your Profile

  • Experience in the technical field / facility management
  • Knowledge in several areas (electrics, plumbing, assembly, painting etc.)
  • Independent, reliable and structured working style
  • Good organisational skills (e.g. warehouse & spare parts)
  • Willingness to work emergency services outside working hours
  • Preferably age in active professional phase
  • Residence in Zurich or surrounding area
  • Driving licence category B
  • Good German skills + basic English knowledge (or vice versa)

💼 We Offer

  • Permanent full-time position (100%)
  • Attractive and performance-based remuneration
  • Modern work equipment & company vehicle
  • Pleasant, collegial working atmosphere
  • Training and development opportunities
  • Long-term perspective in a growing company
  • Clear advancement opportunities within City Pop

💡 Why City Pop? With us, you work in a dynamic environment with real development opportunities. We value quality, teamwork and long-term collaboration.
