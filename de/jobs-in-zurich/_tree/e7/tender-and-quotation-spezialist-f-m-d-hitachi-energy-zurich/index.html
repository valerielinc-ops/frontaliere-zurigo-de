Vollständige Beschreibung

The opportunity

Our product group Insulation & Components at Hitachi Energy is a global technology leader in the design and manufacture of transformer bushings. Renowned international companies across the electrical industry - including power generation, transmission, and distribution - trust our solutions.

To strengthen our operations, we are looking for a Tender and Quotation Specialist to join our team at our MICAFIL bushing factory in Zurich, Switzerland.

You will collaborate closely with our Sales and Engineering teams, as well as other internal functions, to develop competitive tenders based on customer requirements. As each tender brings different needs and challenges, we are looking for someone with technical curiosity, commercial thinking, and a structured approach to their work. If you are passionate about technology, preparing tenders and want to actively contribute, join our team!

How you'll make an impact

  • Analyze requests for quotation (RFQs) and other tender documents to understand customer requirements, scope of work, and technical specifications in close collaboration with the Sales team
  • Collaborate with Sales, Engineering, and other internal departments to gather the necessary information, technical details, and cost estimates for tender submissions
  • Develop comprehensive and well-structured tender and quotation documents, including technical proposals, drawings, and supporting documentation
  • Ensure all submissions are accurate, complete, and meet specified deadlines and submission requirements
  • May participate in pre-bid meetings and customer clarification discussions as required
  • Manage multiple tenders and priorities simultaneously while maintaining high quality and attention to detail
  • Continuously improve tendering processes and tools to enhance efficiency and quality
  • Coordinate with internal stakeholders to develop optimal solutions and proactively maintain clear, structured communication

Your background

  • Technical education and background in the electrical industry or a related field, or equivalent technical experience
  • 3+ years of experience in tendering, bid management, or a technical/industrial environment
  • Confident working at the interface between Sales and Engineering, providing technical support throughout the sales process
  • Ability to evaluate technical proposals, prepare quotations, identify risks, and ensure compliance with customer requirements and quality standards
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Experience with SAP, Salesforce, CRM systems, or configuration tools is considered an advantage
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a keen attention to detail and a solution-oriented mindset
  • Fluency in English is required; proficiency in German or additional languages is an advantage
