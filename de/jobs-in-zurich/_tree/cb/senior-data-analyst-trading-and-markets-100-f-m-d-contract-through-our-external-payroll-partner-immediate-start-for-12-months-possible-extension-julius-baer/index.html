Vollständige Beschreibung

At Julius Baer, we celebrate and value the individual qualities you bring, enabling you to be impactful, to be entrepreneurial, to be empowered, and to create value beyond wealth. Let’s shape the future of wealth management together.

The Data Analyst in GPS Data Analytics team collects, organises, and analyses large data sets to help us structure trading-related data, understand business performance, identify areas for improvement, and inform strategic decision-making. The successful candidate will have a strong foundation in statistical analysis, data visualisation, and data mining techniques, as well as excellent communication and problem-solving skills.

YOUR CHALLENGE

  • Perform data discovery, collect, clean, and pre-process large data sets from various sources, including databases, spreadsheets, and external data providers
  • Develop and maintain databases, data systems, and data processes to support business intelligence and analytics
  • Build an understanding of source systems and data platforms, create and maintain documentation (data dictionaries) for analysed datasets and sources
  • Identify and resolve data quality issues
  • Create data visualisations, reports, and dashboards to communicate findings and insights to stakeholders
  • Analyse and interpret complex data sets to identify trends, patterns, and correlations
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including business stakeholders, product managers, and engineers, to integrate data insights into business decisions
  • Develop and maintain documentation of data analysis processes, methodologies, and results
  • Contribute to project scoping and requirement definition in close collaboration with stakeholders
  • Support Data Engineers in developing and maintaining data pipelines in production

YOUR PROFILE

  • Bachelor's degree in Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, Economics, or related field
  • 5+ years of experience in data analysis, business intelligence, or a related field
  • Strong understanding of financial markets, trading instruments, and market data feeds (e.g., stocks, options, futures, forex)
  • Advanced SQL skills, including complex query development, performance optimisation, data transformation, and analysis of large-scale datasets
  • Proficiency in Excel, and data visualisation tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI)
  • Experience with statistical programming languages such as R, Python
  • Strong understanding of data modelling, data warehousing, and ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes
  • Experience designing and maintaining data models for analytics and reporting
  • Experience working with large data sets using SQL/ Spark/Databricks, etc., to derive actionable insights is highly desirable
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to communicate with stakeholders in various business and technical domains
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively as part of a team
  • Team Player, growth mindset, can-do attitude
  • Fluency in English (verbal/written)
