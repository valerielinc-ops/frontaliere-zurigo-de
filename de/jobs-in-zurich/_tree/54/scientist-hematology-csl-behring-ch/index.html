Vollständige Beschreibung

We are looking for a hands-on Scientist to join our Mechanistic Pharmacology team within Translational Research in Schlieren. This position will focus on developing and applying human in vitro models and ex vivo functional assays to support hematology research programs.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and apply ex vivo functional assay workflows and human in vitro models to support translational research programs.
  • Generate high-quality experimental data to characterize the mode of action of novel therapeutic candidates in hematology indications.
  • Independently perform cell isolation from whole blood, human cell culture, and assay workflows with strong attention to scientific rigor, reproducibility, and data quality.
  • Contribute to the design, optimization, and continuous improvement of experimental approaches and assay performance.
  • Analyze, interpret, and clearly present experimental results to project teams and stakeholders.
  • Maintain accurate documentation of experimental work and support implementation of laboratory best practices and quality standards.
  • Ensure compliance with laboratory safety procedures and contribute to a safe, well-organized, and collaborative working environment.
  • Work closely with scientists across projects and functions, contributing to a highly interactive and team-oriented research setting.

Your Profile

  • Master’s degree in Cell Biology, Life Sciences, or a related scientific discipline, or equivalent relevant laboratory experience; industry experience is an advantage.
  • At least 3 years of practical laboratory experience in cell biology, including strong expertise in human cell culture, working with primary human samples and performing blood-based functional assays.
  • Extensive hands-on experience with flow cytometry required (sample preparation, panel design, acquisition, analysis, and troubleshooting).
  • Experience with hematopoietic stem and progenitor cell cultures, hematopoiesis assays, bone marrow-derived cell models or differentiation systems is highly desirable.
  • Experience in platelet biology, thrombosis/hemostasis research, or translational assays using human blood samples is a strong advantage.
  • Proficiency in data analysis and visualization tools (e.g., FlowJo, GraphPad Prism, Excel).
  • Ability to work independently and take ownership of experiments from study design through execution, analysis, and presentation of results.
  • Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and commitment to high-quality experimental documentation.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
  • Collaborative mindset, flexibility, and ability to work effectively across teams and with different project stakeholders.

About CSL Behring CSL Behring is a global biotherapeutics leader driven by our promise to save lives. Focused on serving patients’ needs by using the latest technologies, we discover, develop and deliver innovative therapies for people living with conditions in the immunology, hematology, cardiovascular and metabolic, respiratory, and transplant therapeutic areas. We use three strategic scientific platforms of plasma fractionation, recombinant protein technology, and cell and gene therapy to support continued innovation and continually refine ways in which products can address unmet medical needs and help patients lead full lives.

CSL Behring operates one of the world’s largest plasma collection networks, CSL Plasma. Our parent company, CSL, headquartered in Melbourne, Australia, employs 32,000 people, and delivers its lifesaving therapies to people in more than 100 countries.
