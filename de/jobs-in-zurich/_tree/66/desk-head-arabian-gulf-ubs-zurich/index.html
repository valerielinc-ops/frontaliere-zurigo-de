Vollständige Beschreibung

Key responsibilities We are seeking a Managing Director to take on the role of Relationship Manager Desk Head for the Arabian Gulf market, based in Zurich. This role carries full leadership responsibility for a senior Relationship Manager desk serving Arabian Gulf clients and represents a key leadership position within Global Wealth Management.

As Desk Head, you are accountable for business performance, people leadership, client outcomes and first line risk oversight. You will set the tone for conduct and quality, act as a role model for UBS values, and contribute actively to market and business strategy.

We’re looking for a Relationship Manager Desk Head to

  • lead, coach and superve a team of Relationship Managers serving Arabian Gulf clients. Define and execute the desk strategy in line with market and regional priorities
  • drive sustainable growth and introduce new business while ensuring full adherence to regulatory requirements and internal policies
  • act as primary escalation point for complex, sensitive or high risk client situations and perform active supervision, including observation of client interactions and structured feedback
  • oversee performance management, talent development and succession planning across the RM desk
  • work closely with investment specialists, product partners and control functions to deliver holistic client solutions
  • represent the Arabian Gulf desk in senior management forums and contributing insights to broader GWM initiatives

Your Career Comeback We are open to applications from career returners. Find out more about our program on ubs.com/careercomeback .

The team You will lead a Zurich based Relationship Manager desk within Global Wealth Management, focused on the Arabian Gulf client segment. The role requires close collaboration with Relationship Managers, assistants, investment specialists and senior stakeholders across Switzerland and international locations to ensure consistent coverage and a high quality client experience.

Your skills and experience

  • extensive experience in wealth management or private banking, with a strong track record in relationship management, ideally 10 years plus
  • senior leadership experience at Director or Managing Director level
  • deep understanding of financial markets, investment solutions and portfolio construction
  • strong knowledge of regulatory frameworks, cross border considerations and first line risk responsibilities
  • proven ability to lead, develop and retain senior Relationship Managers
  • sound judgment and confidence in handling complex client, conduct and reputational matters
  • You’re curious to explore how AI can improve how we build, deliver, and optimize workflows. You do this with sound judgment – validating outputs and aligning with policies, risk standards, and ethical use.
  • fluent in Arabic and English
