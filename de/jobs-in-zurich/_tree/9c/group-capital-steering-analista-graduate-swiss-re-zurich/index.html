Vollständige Beschreibung

Start your journey towards a rewarding career! We’re looking for curious, motivated graduates with strong people skills who want to grow, learn, and make a difference. If you hold a Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD and have up to three years of professional experience, apply for our 18-month Graduate Programme starting on 1 September 2027. You’ll gain hands-on experience in the fast-paced world of reinsurance, rotate into different local teams, and receive tailored training to build your expertise. This is your opportunity to shape your career, help protect societies from the world’s most threatening hazards, and contribute to making the world more resilient. By the end of the programme, you’ll have built the skills, foundations and network to pursue a wide range of career opportunities within Swiss Re. About the Role

As a Graduate in the Group Capital Steering team within Treasury, you will help manage Swiss Re’s capital position and financial flexibility. You will work at the intersection of corporate finance, capital markets, risk and insurance regulation, supporting decisions that influence how the Group allocates and deploys capital.

  • Monitor and analyze Swiss Re's capital and liquidity position, and identify key drivers and emerging trends
  • Contribute to capital planning and forecasting, including market analyses and peer comparisons
  • Perform scenario-based capital impact assessments to evaluate how changes in the economic environment, business performance and/or management actions affect the Group's capital position and financial resilience
  • Support economic steering by assessing risk-adjusted returns to inform capital allocation and sustainable value creation
  • Analyze capital implications of strategic transactions, such as acquisitions, disposals, corporate restructurings and/or large transactions
  • Develop insights and prepare decision material for senior management and governance committees
  • Collaborate across Treasury, Finance, Risk Management, Asset Management and the Business Units, building a broad network and understanding of Swiss Re from day one

About the Team

Group Capital Steering is part of Treasury and helps ensure that Swiss Re maintains a strong and efficient capital position while supporting business growth and shareholder value creation. We work on Group-wide topics with colleagues across Treasury, Finance, Risk Management, Asset Management and the Business Units, giving you broad exposure to how strategic financial decisions are made in a global reinsurance company.

We are a collaborative team that values curiosity, rigorous thinking and open discussion. You will work closely with experienced colleagues, take ownership of meaningful analyses from an early stage and build a broad network across Swiss Re while developing your expertise in capital management and corporate finance.

About You

You are curious, analytical and collaborative, with an interest in understanding how financial and strategic decisions come together. You enjoy working with numbers, can communicate complex topics clearly and are motivated to learn in a fast-paced, international environment. Essential criteria

  • University degree (Bachelor’s, Master’s or PhD) in finance, economics, actuarial science, mathematics, engineering or another relevant discipline, with up to three years of professional experience.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to structure and interpret complex financial information.
  • Advanced proficiency in PowerPoint and Excel
  • Strong written and spoken English skills
  • German is an advantage
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with confidence working across different teams and stakeholders.
  • Proactive, curious and eager to learn in a fast-paced, international environment
  • High level of integrity and professionalism when handling confidential information

Nice to have

  • Professional experience in finance, capital markets and/or (re)insurance
