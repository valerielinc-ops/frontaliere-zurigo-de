Vollständige Beschreibung

At Julius Baer, we celebrate and value the individual qualities you bring, enabling you to be impactful, to be entrepreneurial, to be empowered, and to create value beyond wealth. Let’s shape the future of wealth management together.

Builds and implements Intelligent Automation and Data Analytics solutions for Banking Operations.

The Junior Data Scientist will work on projects that reduce regulatory/operating risks, increase efficiency and scalability.

YOUR CHALLENGE

  • Generate efficiencies and risk reduction in Operational Processes, by designing & building ETL, data science or visualization solutions
  • Intelligent automation: build and implement automation solutions using technical skills and process engineering expertise
  • Project Management: Manage projects of varying duration to implement strategic solutions for Operations in collaboration with Technology

YOUR PROFILE

  • A background in Data Science or Engineering
  • Programming: fluency in Python (or similar), SQL, low code applications (Alteryx, Dataiku or similar)
  • Data Engineering: Strong knowledge and ability in Data Modelling, Data Cleansing, creating and maintaining Data Products
  • Data Visualization: PowerBI, Tableau or similar
  • Machine learning experience: LLMs, NLP, OCR
  • At least two years experience working on Data/ML/AI projects in a corporate environment, ideally in banking
  • Interest in developing Project Management skills – Planning, requirements gathering, documentation, implementation, stakeholder management
  • University Education in Computer Science, Data Science, Data Engineering
  • Process design, process automation, data analytics, data science
