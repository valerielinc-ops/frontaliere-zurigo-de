About the job

As the Data Cloud Sales Specialists team, we work together with Field Sales Representatives (Account Managers), Customer Engineers, and our partners to help our customers modernize their operational and analytical data foundations on GCP. We enable them to transition to modern, unified cloud architectures that position data as the absolute foundation for AI success and power next-generation AI and intelligent applications. As a master of complex lakehouse solutions, data governance, and AI, you are the exact sales expert we need to translate technical capabilities into high-impact business outcomes. You will be at the forefront of driving Google Cloud's most critical data and AI growth. In this role, you will drive growth by securing net-new greenfield logos and identify untapped, uncommitted workloads. You will partner with field sales representatives, customer engineers, and partners to execute joint Data+AI plays that establish long-term data platform standardization. You are a master of value-selling. You will engage with Line-of-Business (LoB) leaders, CDO, CAIO, and CFO to discover critical pain points, build robust business cases (ROI/TCO reduction), and demonstrate the quantifiable business value of modernizing data estates on GCP. Additionally, you will guide technical stakeholders and position Google Cloud's Data Cloud suite as the premium data intelligence platform. You will present demos that showcase how unified, well-governed data foundations power secure, hallucination-free AI agents and real-time experiences. Google Cloud accelerates every organization’s ability to digitally transform its business and industry. We deliver enterprise-grade solutions that leverage Google’s cutting-edge technology, and tools that help developers build more sustainably. Customers in more than 200 countries and territories turn to Google Cloud as their trusted partner to enable growth and solve their most critical business problems.
