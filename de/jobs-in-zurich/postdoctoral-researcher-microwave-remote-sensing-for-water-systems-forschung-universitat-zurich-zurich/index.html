Wichtige Anforderungen

  • You have a PhD degree in remote sensing, physics, geography, environmental science, or a related field.

Bewerbungsprozess

  • We follow a highly interdisciplinary approach ranging from methodological to applicational development and incorporate diverse remote sensing technologies (e.g., optical, thermal, microwave, sound) from in situ to airborne to satellite sensors.
  • We are looking for a highly motivated, creative, and team-oriented postdoctoral researcher in microwave remote sensing with a high interest in studying water systems to strengthen our team.
  • Example research activities of our team include the assessment of local and large-scale forest drought damage caused by climate extremes, the quantification of catchment water balance and surface water dynamics, flood detection, or the 3D exploration of inland waters.
  • You can connect signals with processes and applications.
  • You understand the added value of working with multi-sensor approaches to serve water-related applications.
  • You have excellent written and verbal communication skills in English and (preferably) knowledge of German.
