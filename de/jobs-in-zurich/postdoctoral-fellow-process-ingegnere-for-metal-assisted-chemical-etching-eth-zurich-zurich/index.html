Postdoctoral Fellow / Process Engineer for metal assisted chemical etching

100%, Zurich, fixed-term print Drucken

The X-ray Imaging Professorship is dedicated to advancing the clinical translation of grating-based X-ray interferometry in close collaboration with industrial partners.

  • Postdoctoral Fellow / Process Engineer for metal assisted chemical etching
  • 100%, Zurich, fixed-term print Drucken
  • Your responsibilities
  • Fabricate and process samples on 4-inch silicon wafers using UV photolithography, thin-film deposition, thermal treatments, and metal-assisted chemical etching (MacEtch)

Hauptaufgaben

  • Your responsibilities
  • Fabricate and process samples on 4-inch silicon wafers using UV photolithography, thin-film deposition, thermal treatments, and metal-assisted chemical etching (MacEtch)
  • Perform structural and dimensional characterization using optical microscopy, scanning electron microscopy (SEM), and profilometry
  • Document fabrication procedures, process results, and experimental findings in a structured and traceable manner
  • Analyze data, prepare technical reports, and contribute to project deliverables
  • Present research outcomes at international conferences, scientific symposia, and internal workshops
  • Disseminate results through high-impact scientific publications and collaborative research activities Profile
  • We are seeking a highly motivated professional with a strong collaborative mindset, a hands-on approach to problem solving, and a demonstrated ability to deliver results in a dynamic research environment.
  • PhD in Physics, Chemistry, Materials Science, Engineering, or a closely related discipline
  • Proven experience working in semiconductor cleanroom environments, with a solid understanding of semiconductor processing and hands-on expertise in photolithography and scanning electron microscopy (SEM)

Bewerbungsprozess

  • As part of a multidisciplinary team of academic and industrial researchers, you will contribute to the development and optimization of next-generation fabrication technologies with direct relevance to advanced X-ray imaging applications.
  • The SNSF-funded project ABSOLUTE (n A noassem B led cataly S t and electr O chemica L processing for h U ge aspec T ratio microstructur E s) focuses on developing an innovative catalyst concept for defect control in metal-assisted chemical etching (MacEtch) of silicon.
  • The project aims to overcome key challenges in the fabrication of high-aspect-ratio microstructures by improving process reliability, scalability, and manufacturing quality. Job description
  • In this role, your primary responsibility will be to expand and enhance our MacEtch testing capabilities across a broad range of feature sizes and aspect ratios.
  • You will investigate defect formation mechanisms, optimize process parameters, assess scalability, and contribute to the development of robust fabrication processes that enable the production of complex silicon microstructures for future X-ray optical components.
  • You will contribute to cutting-edge research in:
  • Integration, qualification, and performance assessment of an advanced and unconventional microfabrication process
  • Systematic optimization of fabrication parameters to improve process robustness, reproducibility, and scalability
