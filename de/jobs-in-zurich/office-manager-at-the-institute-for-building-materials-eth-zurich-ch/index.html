  • Personal assistance: Scheduling and calendar management, professional and thoughtful correspondence, coordination with internal and external stakeholders, organization of business travel, coordination within the group, and ensuring efficient workflows

  • A team-oriented mindset with strong interpersonal and communication skills, contributing to a positive and collaborative atmosphere
