Hauptaufgaben

  • Act as the primary Third-Party Data Center Operations (3PDCOPs) representative for all CAP audits, coordinating with local managers to demonstrate site operational resilience.
  • Develop audit plans and verify service provider evidence, like maintenance logs, to ensure critical infrastructure is properly serviced.
  • Partner with the CAP team to address deficiencies, applying risk-based tools to prioritize issues, set remediation deadlines, and ensure compliance.
  • Track drill outcomes and simulations (e.g., ORD/iORD) to document improvements and ensure resilience exercises are executed to standard.
  • Deliver detailed reports and use analytic dashboards to communicate audit trends, site performance, and overall EMEA compliance posture to leadership.

Wichtige Anforderungen

  • Associate's degree, trade school certification, or other certified training in a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • 9 years of experience in technical compliance, facility management, or operations for data centers and mission-critical facilities.
  • Experience with critical data center infrastructure, specifically power distribution, cooling systems, and safety protocols.
  • Experience leading and fronting both internal and external compliance audits for technical facilities.
  • Ability to travel up to 50% across the EMEA region to conduct site visits and audits.
