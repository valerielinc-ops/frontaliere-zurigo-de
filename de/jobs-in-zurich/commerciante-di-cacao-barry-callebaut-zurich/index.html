The Cocoa Trader role is part of the Products Trading team based in Zürich.

The position is primarily responsible for the external sourcing and subsequent sale of cocoa powder and cake to our European manufacturing sites.

In addition, the role supports broader trading activities, including the purchase and sale of cocoa butter and liquor to third-party customers, in line with sales targets and available production capacities.
