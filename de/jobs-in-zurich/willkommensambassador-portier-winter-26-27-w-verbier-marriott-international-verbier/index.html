Unternehmen und Kontext

  • The Hotel Cleanliness Supervisor – Late Shift is responsible for overseeing evening housekeeping operations to ensure cleanliness, safety, and readiness of all assigned rooms, cleanliness of all public area’s extra requests, organization of all housekeeping designated offices and storage rooms.
  • This role supervises housekeeping staff during evening hours, inspects completed work, coordinates shift assignments, and responds to urgent cleaning and delivery requests.
  • Ensure a smooth, detailed and all preparations for handover to shift operations is prepared.
