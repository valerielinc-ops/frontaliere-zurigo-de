Rollenüberblick

Based in Swiss Re's Zurich office, this comprehensive role places you at the heart of our Property & Casualty claims activity for the Mediterranean and Middle East markets.

You'll be both a technical anchor and a market-facing ambassador — analysing complex claims, building and nurturing key client relationships, and contributing to the strategic direction of our portfolio across these dynamic and diverse regions.

This is a role for someone who loves the depth of claims work and the breadth of market engagement.

  • Actively contribute to cross-functional market teams (XFT) , supporting underwriters with your insights and helping to shape market strategy and enhance portfolio profitability.
  • Support the development and promotion of products and solutions tailored to insurance market needs, contributing to Swiss Re's growth agenda.
