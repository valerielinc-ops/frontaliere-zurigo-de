Vollständige Beschreibung

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society. About the role Join Sulzer's Brendale Service Centre and bring your expertise in wheel motor repairs to our dynamic team. We offer competitive workshop pay, excellent workplace culture, career progression, and long-term job security. In this position, you will play a pivotal role in our Wheel Motor Exchange Program. Your main tasks and responsibilities:

  • Overhaul and maintain electric mining haul truck wheel motors and associated equipment.
  • Disassemble, inspect, and repair large mechanical assemblies with precision.
  • Complete job reports and requisition parts to keep projects on track.
  • Replace or repair worn components such as shafts, gears, seals, and bearing fits.
  • Clean and prepare mechanical equipment using specialized methods and tools.
  • Adhere to QESH standards and promote a safe, clean work environment.
  • Support continuous improvement initiatives. To succeed in this role, you will need:
  • Trade Certificate III Mechanical Fitter/Fitter & Turner/Diesel Mechanic (or equivalent)
  • Experience in Mechanical fitting large equipment i.e., Wheel units, gearboxes etc
  • Strong focus on safety and excellent time management skills.
  • Strong communication skills and a collaborative team mindset.
  • Current Driver's License (Forklift license and Dogging ticket are a bonus). What we offer you
  • Competitive hourly rate and annual wage reviews.
  • Opportunities for overtime to boost your earnings.
  • Employee Assistance Program for your wellbeing.
  • On-site parking for convenience.
  • Be part of a global organisation that values innovation, excellence, and its people. Sulzer is an equal opportunity employer. We believe in the strength of a diverse workforce and are committed to offering an inclusive work environment.
