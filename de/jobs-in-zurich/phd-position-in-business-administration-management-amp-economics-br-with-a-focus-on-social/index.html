Unternehmen und Kontext

  • How can we diagnose and overcome resistance to change across individuals, organizations, and societies?
  • How do the effects of social impact programs spread through social systems, and under what conditions do they achieve large-scale, lasting impact?
  • How can behavioral and complexity science be integrated to design pro-grams that are both effective and socially accepted?
  • These are some of the questions motivating our research.
  • The PhD project will develop quantitative empirical studies to understand how social influence processes affect behavioral change at the individual and population level.
  • Ultimately, the research aspires to inform how organizations aiming to achieve positive social impact – whether companies, NGOs, or public institutions – design and implement their actions.
  • Zurich provides a unique ecosystem combining world-class academia with strong industry connections, allowing you to work on impactful research embedded in real-world contexts.
