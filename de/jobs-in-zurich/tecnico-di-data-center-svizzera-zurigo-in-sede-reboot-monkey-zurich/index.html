Wichtige Anforderungen

  • 2+ years of data center or IT infrastructure experience.
  • Hands-on experience in data center operations, including rack-and-stack, server installation, decommissioning, and structured cabling.
  • Strong cabling skills, including Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6a, single-mode and multimode fiber.
  • Familiarity with server hardware (Dell, HP, Supermicro), networking equipment, and physical troubleshooting.
  • Experience with fiber optics, SFP/QSFP transceivers, or cross-connects is an advantage.
  • Ability to lift 50+ lbs and work in raised-floor environments.
  • Ability to work independently, follow technical instructions, and comply with site safety procedures.
  • Own transportation and ability to work on-site in Zurich and nearby locations as required.
  • Availability for occasional night, weekend, and short-notice assignments.
  • Professional communication skills in English.
  • Legally authorized to work in Switzerland without employer sponsorship.
  • Familiarity with DCIM platforms, ticketing systems, and major colocation providers is preferred.
